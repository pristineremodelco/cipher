Privacy Policy

Cipher · Pristine Remodel LLC · Effective [effective date]

Cipher is a calculator, unit converter, set of everyday tools and game score sheets, run by Pristine Remodel LLC. It has no accounts and no server that stores what you do. This page says what Cipher keeps on your device, the outside services it touches, and how to remove it all.

What stays on your device

These are kept in your browser's storage, or in the Android app's own storage. They are never sent to Pristine Remodel LLC or anyone else.

  • Your settings: colors, layout, the cities on your world clock, and the units and currencies you last chose.
  • Your calculation history: the most recent 200 answers, each with the day and time it was worked out, plus up to 100 you chose to save and any notes you added. Also the number held in memory. You can delete entries, clear the history, or turn it off in Settings.
  • Game score sheets in progress and the player names you typed, so a game survives closing the app.
  • The last currency rates Cipher fetched, so conversion still works without a signal.
  • A copy of Cipher's own files, so it opens without a signal.

What you type into the other tools, including Body Metrics and Ovulation, is worked out on your device. It is not saved and not sent anywhere.

Your location

Cipher asks for your location only if you choose to switch colors at sunset and sunrise. It keeps a rough position, rounded to within about half a mile, on your device to work out those times, and never sends it anywhere. While that choice is on and location is allowed, Cipher reads it again when it opens, in case you have travelled. Switching back to the phone's setting forgets it.

Outside services

  • Currency rates come from Frankfurter (frankfurter.dev), a free service that publishes European Central Bank reference rates. Cipher asks it for the day's rates only when you open Currencies. The request carries nothing you typed and no location.
  • The website is served by GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, and the Android app opens the Cloudflare copy.
  • Like any website, each of these sees the IP address of your device when it is asked for something, and may keep it in its own logs.

Cipher has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Sharing

In the Android app, Share sends an answer only to the app you pick.

Backups and removing your data

Cipher does not back anything up, and the Android app is left out of phone backups and transfers to a new phone. Clearing this site's data in your browser, or removing the Android app, removes everything listed above. It cannot be recovered.

Children

[Decision: "Cipher is fine for all ages." or "Cipher is not meant for children under 13."] It collects no personal information from anyone, children included, because nothing you type or save leaves your device.

Changes to this page

When this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. If Cipher ever starts sending anything you type or save off your device, this page will say so before that happens.
