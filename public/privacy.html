Cipher · Pristine Remodel LLC · Effective November 5, 2026

This policy describes what Cipher does with data. Cipher is operated by Pristine Remodel LLC.

No accounts

Cipher has no accounts and no sign-in. There is no server that stores your calculations.

What stays on your device

  • Settings, history, and preferences you choose are stored in this browser (local storage).
  • Nothing you type into the calculator is sent to us.

Outside services

  • Currency conversion rates are fetched from frankfurter.app (European Central Bank data). That request does not include your calculations.
